# Brag Plan: Atenti

## What is this app?

Atenti is a collaborative, geolocated fraud-prevention app for Argentina: search an Alias, CBU, CUIT, phone or local, see community reports and a credibility score, and report scams with evidence and OCR.

## The angle

"Before you transfer, check." A calm, urgent public-safety film: the scam is about to happen, and Atenti is the community that warns you first. Serious, not funny; urgency comes from motion and contrast, not flashing.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

Full-frame dark screen, a red alert ring pulsing, and the line **"¿Te están por estafar?"** — the viewer's own money is at risk.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The search UI with Alias/CBU/CUIT/Tel/Local chips, typing `ju.mp`, and a result card at **80% credibility** with a green light.
- The report UI: upload a receipt, OCR validates the CBU, and the credibility bar fills to **80%**.
- The map/community idea: alerts around you and real scam stories shared by the community.

## Outro / punchline

**"Antes de caer, Atenti."** + the app logo.

## User flow worth showing

Entry: search an identifier → key action: upload evidence and OCR → result: credibility score + alert map. The video shows the product working, not a feature list.

## Tone

- Preset: `app-store`
- Creative direction: "calm, urgent public-safety product film"
- Interpretation: clean cards, confident pace, high contrast; motion carries urgency, copy stays short and readable.

## Format: portrait — 1080x1920

## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `#0F172A`
- Accent: `#DC2626` (alert red)
- Success: `#00E676` (green traffic light)
- Text: `#F8FAFC`
- Muted: `#94A3B8`
- Display font: Inter / system-ui
- Body font: Inter / system-ui
- Strongest visual element: the dark/white/red palette, the search + credibility card, the shield logo.

## Share copy (draft)

Atenti: buscá un Alias, CBU o CUIT antes de transferir. La comunidad que te avisa antes del fraude.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm, confident electronic bed with a subtle lift.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (bundled).
- Music treatment: start at 0, volume 0.8, normalized to −14 LUFS in post; ends under the final logo.
- Music cue guidance: preset cues from the bundled track; strong cues used for the brand reveal and the final logo, no forced beat grid.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; logo/reveal glow may breathe with the music, no waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: light app-store layer; soft hook hit, logo reveal, typing keypresses, result drop, score tick, OCR bell, map pin, final logo bell.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed search, result card reveal, score bar fill, OCR success, map pin, final logo.
- Restraint rule: no sirens or stabs; the urgency is visual and editorial.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s

Dark frame. A red alert ring pulses behind the headline. **"¿Te están por estafar?"** slams in, then the subline **"Antes de transferir, chequeá."**

Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: tension, a low pulse.
Audio-coupled idea: the alert ring breathes with the music's low end (subtle).
Music: warm bed, low intensity.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Brand reveal — 3s

The Atenti shield logo scales in over the dark background. **"Atenti"** and the tagline **"La comunidad que te avisa."** A thin red line draws under the name.

Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: confidence, a small lift.
Audio-coupled idea: logo scale lands near the first strong cue.
Music: bed lifts slightly.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Search the identifier — 5s

Recreate the search experience in a phone card: chips **Alias / CBU / CUIT / Tel / Local**, the text `ju.mp` types into the search bar, then a result card appears: **"Alias: ju.mp"**, **"Estafa Bancaria · 3 reportes"**, **80%** credibility with a green traffic light and a filling bar.

Sequential/interaction: yes — the query reveals left to right, then the result card slides up.
Audio intent: focused, productive.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle key ticks while typing; a card tick when the result lands.
Music: steady bed.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Report with evidence — 5s

Phone card with **"Reportá con evidencias"**, an upload card **"Subir comprobante"**, an OCR confirmation **"✓ OCR validado"**, and the credibility bar filling to **80%**. A small map pin and "Alertas cerca de vos" appear.

Sequential/interaction: yes — upload card, then OCR check, then score.
Audio intent: progress and certainty.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft click for the upload, a confirmation tone for OCR, ticks for the score.
Music: steady, rising slightly.
Transition mood: soft cut → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Outro — 4s

**"Historias de estafas de la comunidad"** as a small badge, then the final line **"Antes de caer, Atenti."** with the logo and a red underline.

Sequential/interaction: yes — badge, then the final line, then the logo.
Audio intent: resolution and call to action.
Audio-coupled idea: final logo hit lands on a strong cue; music fades under the logo.
Music: final swell, then fade.
Transition mood: fade to dark.

**Music mood for this video:** confident, warm, urgent.
**Audio summary:** a steady electronic bed that lifts at the brand reveal and resolves on the final logo, with sparse UI ticks.
